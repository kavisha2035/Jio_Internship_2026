Here is the complete, detailed breakdown of the features implemented in OccuSense AI:

1. Real-Time Computer Vision Engine
YOLOv8 Detection: Leverages a highly optimized YOLOv8 nano model (yolov8n.pt) to detect people and chairs. Frames are resized to a standard width of 640px and processed every 2nd frame (PROCESS_EVERY_N_FRAMES = 2) to maximize performance.
Lightweight Posture Classifier: Employs an aspect-ratio heuristic to differentiate between standing and sitting individuals. This runs in $O(1)$ time with zero CPU overhead, replacing heavy, resource-intensive models like MediaPipe Pose.
Dynamic Proximity Matching: Matches sitting individuals to their closest chair using a perspective-scaled distance: $$\text{Allowed Distance} = \max(45\text{px},, \text{chair_width} \times 1.25)$$ This tightens matching constraints in the background (preventing distant pedestrians from occupying foreground chairs) and relaxes them in the foreground.
Perspective Y-Coordinate Gate: Utilizes 2D image coordinates to identify walking pedestrians. If a person's feet (person.y2) are lower in the frame than the chair's base (chair.y2) by more than 35% of the chair's height, they are classified as walking in the foreground aisle and ignored.
Virtual Chair Synthesis: If a sitting person is detected but their chair is 100% blocked from YOLO's view by their body, the pipeline synthesizes a virtual chair box underneath them. This guarantees the occupant is always associated with a seat.
2. State Stabilization & Hysteresis Filters
Asymmetric Hysteresis State Smoothing: Handles temporary frame drops and posture misclassifications:
Vacant $\rightarrow$ Occupied (Fast transition): Requires 3 consecutive frames (~0.5 seconds) of detected occupancy to activate.
Occupied $\rightarrow$ Vacant (Slow transition): Requires 15 consecutive frames (~2.5 seconds) of consistent vacancy to release, absorbing momentary walks away or shifts in posture.
Total Chair Count Smoother: Uses a rolling queue max filter over the last 30 frames to stabilize the total seat count on the dashboard, preventing the count from dropping when chairs are temporarily blocked by passersby.
3. Occupancy Dwell Tracker
Session Monitoring: Tracks continuous seating durations for every individual chair.
Database Logging: Once an occupant leaves a chair, the seat's dwell session (chair ID, start time, end time, duration in minutes) is logged to the SQLite database.
Dwell Time Display: Active seating durations (e.g. OCCUPIED 2h 15m) are displayed live on both the video feed labels and the scrollable cards on the dashboard.
4. Interactive Occupancy Heatmap
Decaying Coordinate Grid: Accumulates coordinate coordinates of sitting persons onto a dedicated matrix that slowly decays at a rate of 0.97 per frame.
OpenCV Heatmap Visualization: Standardizes and applies a color map (COLORMAP_JET) over the video stream, togglable directly from the dashboard header.
5. Analytics & SQL Database Core
Startup Space Allocation: Maintains tables mapping desk spaces to contract agreements for registered startups (e.g. Startup Alpha, Startup Beta).
Startup Space Utilization Report: Generates space optimization statistics by comparing contracted desks against actual hourly desk usage logs.
Automated Renewal Recommendations: Recommends action plans during lease renewals:
High utilization: Recommend contract renewal or space expansion.
Medium utilization: Recommend renewal with a downsize option.
Zero/Low utilization: Recommend reducing allocated spaces or non-renewal.
Peak Occupancy Analysis: Calculates hourly occupancy rates throughout the day.
6. Premium Glassmorphic Web Dashboard
WebSocket Live Stream: Delivers real-time annotated frame streams, counts, and active dwell durations over WebSockets.
Live Feed Tab: Displays a glowing status header (Connected pill), big metric counter cards, the main video feed with a togglable heatmap switch, and a scrollable checklist of detected chairs showing active status indicators.
Reports & Charts Tab:
Peak Occupancy SVG Bar Chart: A high-performance chart that plots average occupancy rates hourly without external heavy graphing packages.
Utilization Table: Displays contract counts, actual desk usage, utilization percentages, and color-coded recommendation badges.
Dwell Duration Report: Shows average occupancy session durations per chair.
